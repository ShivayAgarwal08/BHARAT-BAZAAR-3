# Bharat Bazaar — 5-minute SIH demo

Use the dedicated **SIH DEMO 2026** Artisan, Student, and Admin accounts. Their passwords remain only in the ignored local `.env.demo` file.

## 0:00–0:30 — The problem

Traditional artisans bring craft expertise but may not have the time or digital-business support to grow online. College students have useful digital skills but need real work and a portfolio. **“Artisans create. Students grow. We connect.”** Bharat Bazaar is the coordination and trust layer between them.

## 0:30–1:15 — Artisan experience

Sign in as the demo artisan. Show the simple sidebar: **Home, My Manager, Find a Manager, My Growth, My profile, Help**. Switch the language control to Hindi. Open **Help** to explain the live assisted-registration request/callback flow. AI-assisted calling, BHASHINI, and Exotel are roadmap items, not current claims.

## 1:15–2:00 — Sponsored pilot

Open **My Growth**, then **View pilot history**, and open the completed _SIH DEMO 2026: sponsored pilot_. State the economics exactly: **Platform-sponsored pilot; artisan cost: ₹0; student stipend: sponsored by Bharat Bazaar.** Show approved work and the growth measurements. Say **“Change during this engagement”**—the records do not prove that the student caused every business outcome.

## 2:00–2:40 — Student experience

Sign in as the demo student. Show **My Artisan** for the current engagement and **Portfolio** for the completed pilot. Explain the student receives practical experience, a verified completed-project record, reviews where available, and a route to future paid work. In the pilot, the stipend is funded by Bharat Bazaar—not by the artisan.

## 2:40–3:30 — Marketplace and paid engagement

Sign back in as the artisan and open **Find a Manager**. Show the verified student profile: skills, availability, languages, rating, and expected monthly rate; private contact details stay hidden. Explain that a marketplace interest request does not itself create a contract. The student accepts the request, discovery is submitted and reviewed by an admin, then a paid contract can be proposed and accepted. Show the active _SIH DEMO 2026: artisan-paid follow-up_ as the later, artisan-paid engagement.

## 3:30–4:10 — External payment record

On the paid engagement, show the payment record. Explain: Bharat Bazaar does **not** hold or move money. The artisan pays externally; the platform records the payment method/reference or proof where applicable, student confirmation, and platform verification. The demo record is a verified external cash payment.

## 4:10–4:45 — Admin coordination

Sign in as Admin. Show student verification, the tagged growth request, candidate matching, the submitted discovery report and its admin review, the sponsored pilot via the growth-request detail, the later paid contract, payment oversight, and completion information. Bharat Bazaar remains involved as the coordination and trust layer.

## 4:45–5:00 — Close

“Artisans already have the skill to create. Students already have the basic digital skills to help. Bharat Bazaar gives both sides the connection, trust and structure they are missing.”

## Live workflow truth

The implemented discovery sequence is: **student submits discovery → admin reviews discovery → contract proceeds**. Artisan discovery confirmation/changes is a future workflow, not a live claim.
