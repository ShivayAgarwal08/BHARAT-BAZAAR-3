# Bharat Bazaar — judge Q&A

1. **Why will artisans use this?** It gives a local business a structured path to practical digital help without treating the artisan as a one-off freelancer client.
2. **Why will students work for artisans?** They gain verified hands-on work, a portfolio, feedback, and a route to paid engagements.
3. **How does Bharat Bazaar make money?** The MVP demonstrates the trust and coordination workflow; commercial pricing is a future operating-model decision, not a live revenue claim.
4. **Who pays the student in the first assignment?** Bharat Bazaar sponsors the pilot stipend. The artisan’s cost is ₹0.
5. **What happens after the free pilot?** An artisan can browse verified student managers, send interest, and choose a later artisan-paid engagement after discovery and contract acceptance.
6. **How is this different from Fiverr or Upwork?** Bharat Bazaar stays involved in verification, matching, discovery review, agreements, work records, completion, and issue handling.
7. **What stops students from cheating artisans?** Admin verification, structured discovery, contracts, task records, participant-only access, completion review, disputes, and reviews add accountability.
8. **What happens if revenue does not improve?** Growth measurements are recorded as change during the engagement, not promised results; the parties can review work, completion, and disputes through the platform process.
9. **How are language barriers solved?** English and Hindi are live. Human-assisted registration is available; voice and BHASHINI support are roadmap items.
10. **Why do you need AI/BHASHINI later?** They can make onboarding and support more accessible at scale, but are not required for the live workflow today.
11. **How will this scale?** The role-based, workflow-driven foundation lets verified students and operational admins coordinate many local engagements with consistent records.
12. **Why are payments external?** The MVP avoids holding customer funds. It records payment evidence and confirmation while participants pay directly.
13. **How is student performance measured?** Through discovery, agreed deliverables, task status, progress records, completion, and eligible two-way reviews.
14. **How are artisans protected?** Profiles are verified where applicable; admins coordinate matching and discovery; contracts, task approval, payment records, and disputes create an auditable path.
15. **Why is this useful for rural India?** It connects craft businesses to nearby or reachable digital capability while keeping language, trust, and low-friction assisted entry in view.
16. **What is the core innovation?** A managed artisan–student growth relationship: not just discovery, but verification, coordinated discovery, structured work, evidence, and progression from a sponsored pilot to paid engagement.
17. **What is implemented versus roadmap?** Accounts, multilingual UI, operational matching/discovery review, contracts, tasks, metrics, marketplace, portfolio, and external-payment records are live. AI calling, Exotel, BHASHINI, automated voice onboarding, advanced matching AI, artisan discovery confirmation, and automated stipend payout are roadmap.
