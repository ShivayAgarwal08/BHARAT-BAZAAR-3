# Bharat Bazaar — SIH feature status

## LIVE NOW

- Separate Artisan, Student Growth Manager, and Admin accounts with server-side role controls.
- English and Hindi interface, including the demo’s core artisan and student screens.
- Artisan business-help and assisted-registration request forms.
- Admin review of growth requests, candidate matching, assignment handling, and student verification.
- Student discovery submission and **admin** discovery review.
- Versioned sponsored-pilot and artisan-paid contracts with independent acceptance.
- Sponsored pilot presentation: artisan cost is ₹0 and student stipend is sponsored by Bharat Bazaar.
- Work tasks, approvals, completion workflow, and recorded business-growth measurements.
- Verified student marketplace profiles, interest requests, and a completed-project portfolio.
- External-payment records, private proof authorization, student receipt confirmation, disputes, and review/completion foundations where the relevant contract state permits them.

## MVP SIMULATION / MANUAL

- A human handles assisted-registration callback follow-up after the request is submitted.
- Bharat Bazaar’s sponsored pilot stipend is represented and coordinated by the platform; automated payout is not implemented.
- Admin review is the live trust and matching process; it is not automated matching AI.
- The demo uses clearly tagged SIH DEMO 2026 records to show a completed sponsored pilot and a later external artisan-paid engagement.

Bharat Bazaar does not process or hold participant money. For artisan-paid work, participants pay outside the platform and Bharat Bazaar records evidence, confirmation, and status only.
